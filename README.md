## Understanding Divorce Matters

Divorce proceedings may broadly involve mutual consent or contested proceedings.

In a mutual consent matter, both parties generally agree to proceed with the divorce and work towards resolving connected issues such as maintenance, custody and financial settlement.

A contested divorce may involve disagreement over the divorce itself or related issues and can require further proceedings before the appropriate court.

## Documents Commonly Required

The documents required depend on the facts of the matter. They may include:

- marriage-related records
- identity and address documents
- income and financial records
- correspondence between the parties
- documents relating to children
- settlement communications
- records supporting specific claims or responses

Reviewing these documents at an early stage can help clarify the issues that may need attention during the proceedings.
